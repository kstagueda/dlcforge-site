DLCForge privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who uses DLCForge

DLCForge is an in-house tool of Modifications by Eikmann. It runs on the owner's own PC and is used only by the owner. It has no other users and no website accounts.

What it can see in your Google account, and why

  • Google Drive, only the files DLCForge made. It saves locked backup copies of the studio's own records, a restore guide and a copy of the app in a folder called "DLCForge Backups". Once a week it downloads the newest copy to check that it can be restored. It cannot see, change or delete any other file in the Drive.
  • Google Sheets. It writes the owner's own business figures into the owner's own spreadsheet.

What it never does

  • It never shares, sells or transfers Google data to anyone, and never uses it for advertising.
  • It never sends Google data to the developer or to any server other than Google's own.
  • Backup copies are locked with AES-256 encryption, using a passphrase only the owner knows, before they leave the PC.

Where the data is kept

In the owner's own Google Drive and Google Sheet, and on the owner's PC. The sign-in that lets DLCForge reach Google is stored only on that PC.

Removing access

Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services → DLCForge → Delete all connections. The backup copies stay in the Drive until they are deleted there.

Google API Services User Data Policy

DLCForge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page changes, with a new date at the top.
